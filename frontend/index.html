Our Heritage

Northern Elegance, Tailored For Today

ABBAHGAMJI is built on the tailoring language of Northern Nigeria — the drape of an agbada, the clean line of a senator wear, the ease of a jallabiya. We keep that language and refine the fit, the fabric, and the finish.

Every piece can be cut to your exact measurements. Add your body measurements at checkout — we call it your tailor's inscription — and our tailors cut for you, not for a rack size.

How It's Made

The Fitting

From your first message to a garment on your shoulders — four stages, every one done by hand.

01

Consultation

Tell us the silhouette, fabric and occasion — in person, by phone or on WhatsApp.
